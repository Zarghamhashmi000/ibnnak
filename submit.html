My submissions

How it works

1. You submit. Your material is saved as Under review — it does not appear on the public site yet.
2. Admin reviews in the dashboard. If a new category is needed, admin creates it on approval, so your work lands in its own specific category.
3. Approved → published under your author name, with a landing page at author.html?author=Your Name listing all your approved books and articles.
4. Rejected → you see why. The admin's message shows on your submission here. You can fix it and submit again.

Files: paste a link to your book PDF / article file (Google Drive, Cloudflare R2 link, or another public link). On approval, admin moves the file to R2 / site storage and sets the final read and download links — that is why nothing publishes automatically. If you have an R2 Worker upload URL from the admin, it can be added later; the link field works today without it.
