1. Terms of Use

What Ibnnak is. Ibnnak is a read-online digital library. Visitors can read books free on this site. There are no visitor accounts and no sign-up is needed to read.

Fair use. Use the library for personal reading and study. Do not misuse the site, try to break it, scrape it in bulk, or use it to harm others.

Content may change. Books, articles, quotations and videos may be added, corrected or removed at any time, without notice. The owner may change these terms; the version on this page is the current one.

No warranty. The library is provided as it is. While care is taken to keep books readable and correct, Ibnnak does not guarantee that every file is complete, error-free, or always available.

2. Reading & Downloading

Reading is free. Every book on Ibnnak can be read online, free, on the site itself. No price and no download button appear on the home page, the books list, or on book cards.

Downloading is separate. Downloading is handled only on each book's own download page. Some books are free to download; some are paid. Any price is shown only on that download page, never on the front of the site.

Your copy. A downloaded copy is for your personal use. Do not re-upload, resell, or publish it as your own work.

3. Submissions & Rights

Your own work only. If you submit a book, article, quotation or video, you confirm it is your own work, or that you have permission to publish it here, and that it is not copied from another author.

Approval first. Nothing publishes automatically. Every submission goes to the admin dashboard for review. Approved work is published under the author name you provide, in its own category, with its own author landing page. Rejected work is not published, and the admin's reason is shown to you on the Submit page.

What admin may do. On approval, admin may place your work in the right category, create a category for it if needed, and set the final read and download links. Admin may also remove published work at any time if a rights problem is found.

4. Privacy

No accounts, no tracking by Ibnnak. Ibnnak does not ask visitors to create an account, and does not collect personal data from readers. Reading on the site does not require your name, email, or any other personal detail.

Saved books stay on your device. If you save a book, that list is stored only in your own browser (localStorage). It is not sent to Ibnnak, and it does not follow you to another phone or browser.

Submissions. If you submit work, the name, author name, and email (if you give one) that you type into the form are used only to review your submission and to tell you whether it was approved or rejected. They are not sold or shared.

Other services. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Firebase (Firestore) to store books, articles and submissions. Those services have their own privacy policies and may log basic technical data, such as IP addresses, as part of running the service.

5. Copyright & Takedown

Respect for authors. Ibnnak only publishes work the owner has the right to distribute, or work submitted by its author (or with the author's permission) and approved by admin. If you believe a book or other work on Ibnnak infringes your copyright, you can ask for it to be removed.

How to request a takedown. Send a request through the Submit page, marked as a takedown request, and include: (1) the title and the exact Ibnnak page link of the work, (2) proof that you own the rights or act for the owner, (3) your name and an email where the owner can reply, and (4) a short statement that you believe, in good faith, the work is published without permission.

What happens next. The owner reviews the request. If it is valid, the work is removed or disabled promptly, and it will no longer be readable or downloadable on Ibnnak. If the request is incomplete, the owner may ask for the missing details before acting.

Removed work. A removed book is not restored unless the rights question is resolved with the owner.

6. Contact

For questions about these terms, a takedown request, or a correction to a book, contact the owner through the Submit page. For submitting your own work, use the same page — your submission goes to the admin dashboard for approval first.
